Why these five

ADP wins year one on every line. It loses the two-year comparison — $119,331 against TriNet's $116,525 — for one reason: its plan year ends 6/1/2027, so Onyx gets an eight-month first year and absorbs two increases inside 24 months where every other vendor absorbs one. Separately, three of ADP's cost lines rest on our reading rather than on their confirmation: workers' compensation at $0, what the 1.53% is actually charged on, and whether we may keep the cheapest plan on the menu to hold the $187.02 floor.
